~~~markdown
![Adventure Tools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri/main/docs/images/banner.png)

**Everything an explorer needs.** Hover any tool and hold **Shift** in-game to see how it works. You also get a guide book on your first join.

![Items](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri/main/docs/images/items_en.png)

## ⚔️ 3D Maneuver Gear
Attack on Titan style! **Left click** fires the left hook, **right click** the right one. Hooks pull you in, two hooks sling you through the air and **Space** boosts with gas. Gas canisters in your inventory load automatically.

## 🪝 Grappling Hooks
Iron, diamond and netherite. Pull yourself to walls, hold **Shift** to swing (Space climbs the rope, S goes down), or hook mobs and items to pull them to you. No fall damage while pulled.

## 🪂 Glider
Hold it while falling to glide; it opens above your head. Fly over a **lit campfire** and hot air lifts you up.

## 👢 Double Jump Boots
Jump again in mid-air. Fall damage is halved.

## 🎒 Backpacks
27 to 54 slots (leather, iron, gold, diamond; contents kept on upgrade). Wear it on your back, where it's visible, and open it with **B**. Add a **Pickup Module** so items you already carry go straight into the bag.

## 🧭 More tools
- **Rope Ladder**: unrolls down cliffs, picks up in one click
- **Zipline Kit**: string a line between two blocks and ride it
- **Signal Flare**: a minute of light and red signal smoke
- **Prospector's Spyglass**: ores glow through walls
- **Teleport Compass**: save a spot, teleport back
- **Magnet Ring**: pulls items and XP (toggle with **M**)
- **Climbing Gloves**: climb any wall
- **Sleeping Bag**: sleep anywhere without moving your spawn
- **Death Compass**: leads you back to your items after dying

## ✨ Also
- Found in **structure chests** and sold by the **wandering trader**
- **4 enchantments:** Reach, Quick Pull, Soaring, Attraction
- **10 advancements**
- **Server config**, recipe book support (and JEI / EMI)
- Optional **Curios** support
- **English** and **Turkish** translations

## Installation
Install **NeoForge** for Minecraft **26.2** and put the jar in your `mods` folder. On multiplayer the mod is required on **both the server and every client**.

📖 [Full guide with all recipes](https://github.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri#readme) · [Türkçe kılavuz](https://github.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri/blob/main/README.tr.md)
~~~
