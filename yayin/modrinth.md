# Macera Aletleri – Adventure Tools

![Items](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri/main/docs/images/items_en.png)

Everything an explorer needs. Hover any tool and hold **Shift** in-game to see how it works — and you get a guide book on your first join.

## ⚔️ 3D Maneuver Gear
Attack on Titan style! **Left click** fires the left hook, **right click** the right one. Hooks pull you in, two hooks sling you through the air, **Space** boosts with gas. Carry gas canisters — they load automatically.

## 🪝 Grappling Hooks (Iron / Diamond / Netherite)
Pull yourself to walls, hold **Shift** to swing like a pendulum (Space climbs the rope, S goes down), or hook mobs and items to yank them to you. No fall damage while pulled.

## 🪂 Glider
Hold it while falling to glide — it opens above your head. Fly over a **lit campfire** and hot air lifts you up, Breath of the Wild style!

## 👢 Double Jump Boots
Jump again in mid-air. Also halve fall damage.

## 🎒 Backpacks
27 to 54 slots (leather → iron → gold → diamond, contents kept on upgrade). **Wear it on your back** (you'll see it!) and open with **B**. Add a **Pickup Module** so items you already carry go straight into the bag.

## 🧭 Exploring
- **Rope Ladder** – click a cliff edge, it unrolls to the ground. Pick the whole thing up in one click.
- **Zipline Kit** – string a line between two blocks and ride it.
- **Signal Flare** – light up caves for a minute and mark the spot with red smoke.
- **Prospector's Spyglass** – ores glow through walls, each in its own color.
- **Teleport Compass** – save a spot, teleport back.
- **Magnet Ring** – pulls items and XP (toggle with **M**).
- **Climbing Gloves** – climb any wall.
- **Sleeping Bag** – sleep anywhere without moving your spawn point.
- **Death Compass** – given when you respawn, leads you to your dropped items.

## ✨ Also
- Tools can be found in **dungeon, temple, mineshaft and other structure chests**, and sold by the **wandering trader**
- **4 new enchantments:** Reach, Quick Pull, Soaring, Attraction
- **10 advancements** — become *Tarzan* or a *Titan Slayer*
- **Server config** (also in-game via Mods → Config)
- Recipes in the **recipe book** (and JEI / EMI)
- Optional **[Curios](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios)** support: magnet ring, gloves and glider in accessory slots
- Full **English** and **Turkish** translations

## Installation
1. Install **NeoForge** for Minecraft **26.2**.
2. Put the jar in your `mods` folder.
3. On multiplayer, the mod is required on **both the server and every client**.

📖 **Full guide with all recipes:** [GitHub README](https://github.com/KULLANICI/macera-aletleri#readme)
